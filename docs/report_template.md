## Data Privacy Requirements

- Generate a non-identifying Report ID instead of extracting the applicant's name for the report.
- Do not intentionally include names, home addresses, telephone numbers, email addresses or dates of birth in the report.
- Use professional experience, skills, qualifications and achievements for personalisation.
- Do not upload applicant CVs into the shared Pinecone knowledge base.
- Minimise personal information sent to external AI services where technically feasible.
- Configure n8n execution-history settings to minimise retention of CV files and extracted text.
- Inform applicants about CV processing and relevant external service providers before submission.
