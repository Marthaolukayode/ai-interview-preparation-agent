# GTM — Future Sprint Experiments

## Objective

Evaluate whether the AI Interview Preparation Agent provides useful, personalised interview preparation and identify opportunities for future product improvement.

## Experiment 1 — User Feedback

**Hypothesis:** Job seekers will find interview preparation reports more useful when the recommendations reflect their CV, target company and job description.

**Approach:** Ask 3–5 volunteer users to test the agent using synthetic or anonymised CVs and sample job descriptions.

**Success Metric:** At least 80% of testers rate the report as useful (4/5 or higher).

## Experiment 2 — Report Quality Comparison

**Hypothesis:** Interview preparation reports using verified company information will be more relevant than generic AI-generated interview questions.

**Approach:** Compare reports generated with company research and RAG against generic interview questions for the same role.

**Success Metric:** At least 70% of testers prefer the research-supported report.

## Experiment 3 — User Experience

**Hypothesis:** A simple form-based interface will make the agent accessible to users without technical knowledge.

**Approach:** Observe 3–5 testers completing the form and generating an interview preparation report.

**Success Metric:** At least 80% complete the process without assistance.

## Future Improvements

Based on experiment results, future sprints could improve report personalisation, company research accuracy, usability and delivery options.
